Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 November 2026

Introduction

WoollyRoad ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application to translate knitting and crochet patterns.

Information We Collect

We collect the following information:

  • Pattern Images: The photos and PDF pages you choose to translate. They are sent for translation only when you start a translation
  • Projects and Translations: Your projects, pattern images, and translation results, stored only on your device
  • Device Identifier: A random identifier created by the app and stored in your iCloud Keychain, used to keep your credits when you reinstall the app. It is not linked to your name, email address, or Apple Account unless you email us and keep the user ID the app adds to your message
  • Credit and Usage Records: Your credit balance, the number of translations you make, and technical details of each translation request (for example, its size and processing cost), linked to your device identifier
  • Purchase Information: Records of credit purchases and refunds (product, transaction identifier, and date), processed via Apple's StoreKit. We never receive your payment details
  • Usage Data: How you use the app (for example, which screens you view and which features you use), collected through Firebase Analytics
  • Crash Data: Technical information about app crashes (for example, device model and iOS version), collected through Firebase Crashlytics
  • Support Emails: If you email us, we receive your email address, your message, and the user ID and app version that the app adds to the email. You can remove these details before sending

WoollyRoad does not require an account and does not collect your name or contacts, or your email address unless you email us. The app does not show ads.

How We Use Your Information

Your information is used to:

  • Provide and maintain the WoollyRoad service
  • Translate the pattern images you choose
  • Keep track of your credits and apply credit purchases and refunds
  • Prevent abuse of free credits
  • Understand how the app is used and fix crashes so we can improve it
  • Answer your support requests

Translations

Translations are generated by Claude, a service provided by Anthropic. When you start a translation, and only after you agree in the app, the pattern images you chose are sent through our server to Anthropic. We do not send your device identifier or any other information that identifies you to Anthropic.

Anthropic does not use the images sent through our service to train its models. We do not store your pattern images or translation results on our server. They are kept only on your device.

Pattern images may contain whatever appears in your photos, so please avoid including personal details you do not wish to share.

Data Storage

Your projects, pattern images, and translations are stored locally on your device. Your credit balance, usage records, and purchase records are stored on Firebase (Google), linked to your device identifier.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information. We share information only with the third-party services listed below, and only as needed to run the app.

Third-Party Services

WoollyRoad uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase (Google): Authentication, storage of credits and usage records, server functions, App Check, analytics, and crash reporting
  • Anthropic (Claude): Translating pattern images
  • Apple: In-app purchase processing, App Attest, and iCloud Keychain

Please review Google's Privacy Policy at: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Please review Anthropic's Privacy Policy at: https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy

Please review Apple's Privacy Policy at: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy

Data Retention

Data stored on your device is removed when you delete the app. Credit, usage, and purchase records are kept so that your credits remain available if you reinstall the app, and to meet our accounting obligations. You may request deletion of these records at any time by contacting us.

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access your personal data
  • Correct inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of your data
  • Use the app without creating an account

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
